# Camper trailer: research brief

This is the brief the five research reports in this folder were written against. It records the project, the decisions made before the research started, and the rules used to choose products.

## The project

A one-off off-road camper trailer that the owner designs and builds himself in Australia. It is towed by a Porsche Cayenne GTS (3,500 kg braked towing limit, about 140 kg towball download). The owner is a skilled mechanic and fabricator. The trailer will be built to VSB1 (the Australian government guide for building light trailers up to 4.5 t ATM) and the ADRs, inspected for registration, and reviewed by an engineer. Most of its life is highway towing, plus grass, desert tracks, fire trails, light mud and shallow river crossings. No rock crawling.

Decisions so far:

- External body width 2,400 mm, a hard limit. Walls are 30-35 mm foam-core panels, so the interior is about 2,330 mm wide.
- Body length 6,000 mm at most. The target is about 5,000 mm to save weight, so every module should be as short as it can be.
- Interior height with the roof collapsed: 2,000 mm at most, target 1,800 mm, measured from the garage floor. A raising roof (probably hydraulic) will add headroom at camp. Its design is deferred, but everything must fit with the roof down.
- Galvanised steel chassis (out of scope for now). Body in fibreglass-skinned foam-core sandwich panels. Aluminium for bolted-on parts. Treat the garage floor as flat, with no wheel arches inside the garage.

## Scope of the research: the garage and the bed

Two motorcycles travel inside a garage, with a queen bed above them.

| Bike | Weight, fully fuelled | Length | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Triumph Street Triple 765, stripped for track use | 175 kg | 2,055 mm | 792 mm | 1,064 mm |
| Aprilia Tuareg 660 Rally | 206 kg | 2,220 mm | 965 mm | 1,370 mm |

These figures come from the owner and override any published figure. Where a source disagrees, the reports note the difference but keep the owner's figures.

Working layout at the time of the research: the bikes stand across the trailer (their length runs along the 2,330 mm interior width). They are loaded nose first through a door in the side wall, so both face the same way. The garage is roughly 1,600-2,000 mm long along the trailer. A queen mattress sits on a platform above them, and the platform's underside must clear the Tuareg's 1,370 mm top plus a margin. With the roof down there is little room above the mattress. The raised roof provides sitting room. The design that followed the research is in `DESIGN.md`.

## How products were chosen

In this order:

1. Reliability and a record of hard use (enclosed race trailers, toy haulers, marine, overland caravans).
2. Parts and service availability, in Australia if possible.
3. Serviceability: can it be repaired or have parts swapped, ideally in the field.
4. Then the lightest product that passes 1-3.

Price was ignored.

## What was recorded for every product

Name, maker, model and part number, what it does here, quantity, unit and total weight (kg), the dimensions that matter (mm), its rating where relevant (working load limit, breaking strength, load capacity), a link to the maker's main product page, an Australian stockist link if one exists, and one line on reliability and serviceability.

Weights and dimensions come from spec sheets, manuals or the maker's pages, with the URL next to the value. Estimates are labelled as estimates and show how they were worked out (for example 6061 aluminium at 2.70 g/cm3 times the volume). Each report ends with its open questions and a list of the claims and links it could not verify.
