## Pipe schedule

Each pipe and hose of `data/routes.yaml`, routed on the solved layout by `cad/routes.py`. PEX turns at a push-fit
elbow where its 130 mm clipped radius does not fit, and the DWV wastes turn only at bends, so each counts its
fittings. Every gravity waste falls at least 1:50 to the grey tank under the wet room. Wrap or All columns adds each
run's system, mass and price.

The services checks for every cable and pipe are on the <a href={withBase('/electrical/#services-checks')}>Electrical page</a>.

- Fresh: two welded HDPE tanks between the rails over the axles hold 420 L. At most {t.params.water_travel_l} L ride while towing
  (decisions H13 and H21), and the rest goes in at camp through the Puretec CR45 filter, by hose or the creek transfer pump,
  with a chlorine dose.
- Supply: a Shurflo Trail King 7 at 2.1 bar with an accumulator feeds every outlet. The drinking tap has its own
  Puretec CR-Z2 filter and an Acuva UV purifier. Hot water comes from the Truma Combi D 6 E's 10 L boiler through a
  50 °C mixing valve.
- Shower: the DauerShower 2.0 cleans and reheats the same 5 L, so a 20 min shower uses about 5 L. Its switching unit
  sits in a sealed, insulated well under the shower floor.
- Grey: every drain falls by gravity to a {W.grey_tank_l} L tank in the rear bay, with a lever valve and a camlock on
  the kerb side. It travels empty, and a 40 L wheeled tote serves longer stays.
- Over the battery: no water, waste or diesel line passes within 300 mm of the battery's terminal face, but the
  washer's sleeved hoses (SVC-04, AS/NZS 3001.2). The washer's and the dryer's lines cross over the battery box under
  their base, so a 1.5 mm aluminium drip tray on the box's lid (EL-09) catches anything they drip into a cone widening
  1 in 5 over the face, the trailer parked up to about 11 degrees off level (est.).
- Heat: the Combi's hot outlet, before the mixing valve, keeps 25 mm from every extra-low-voltage cable and 50 mm
  from the 230 V, and the diesel line keeps 50 mm (est.) from the exhaust behind the Combi (SVC-11, Truma's installation
  instructions).
