This is the first layout of the whole trailer. It places the <a href={withBase('/garage-bed/')}>garage and bed
module</a> over the axles and puts every other zone and system around it as blocks. Each block is an envelope with
a mass, not a design. The point is to find where the axles go, what the car carries on its towball, and what the
chassis has to be. The design notes are in `TRAILER.md` in the repository, and drawing CT-001 is the general
arrangement.

The project datum in research report 02 measures X rearward from the ball, Y towards the kerb side and Z up. That
is a left-handed frame, and CAD programs are right-handed, so this model is the mirror image of the real trailer:
the garage door shows on the right when you look from behind. Every number is unaffected. Drawing CT-001 mirrors
the model back, so its views show the trailer as it will be seen.

## The layout and why

From front to back there are the drawbar box with the generator, gas and fuel, then the front utility zone, the
garage with the bed above it, and the living zone. The front zone holds the kitchen slide-out with the wardrobe
over it, the washer-dryer, the chest freezer, the diesel heater and hot water unit, and the inverter and
chargers. It has its own door in the road-side wall. The living zone holds the fridge, the 1 x 1 m wet bathroom
with its recirculating shower, the desk, the air-conditioner under the desk, and the entry door in the kerb-side
wall. The water tanks sit between the rails over the axles, so the towball barely changes as the water is used,
and the 12 kWh battery sits just behind them.

The floor height comes from the running gear in report 06. The tyre is 737.7 mm across, the hubs sit at
{n(ch.hub_z)} mm, and the XT suspension rises 60 mm at full bump. With 50 mm of clearance above the tyre, the floor
underside is at {n(ch.floor_underside_z)} mm and the garage floor surface at {n(ch.floor_z)} mm. The rails are
215 mm above the hubs, at {n(ch.rail_underside_z)} mm, so a {n(ch.raiser_h)} mm raiser fills the gap from the rail
top to the floor.

The axle group centre is {n(P.axle_x)} mm behind the ball. The build solved it for {n(t.target_kg)} kg on the ball
fully loaded, the target from report 07, and moved the module, the tanks, the battery and the suspension with it.
The living zone stopped the garage {n(P.axle_x - P.module_x0 - P.mod_bikes_cog_x)} mm short of sitting exactly
over the axles, so taking both bikes and their kit out changes the towball by only
{n(tc('TC2').nominal.towball_kg - tc('TC4').nominal.towball_kg)} kg.

The drawbar is {n(ch.drawbar_length)} mm long from the ball to the body. At a 90 degree jack-knife the drawbar box
then clears the Cayenne's side by {n(ch.jackknife_box_mm)} mm and the body corners by {n(ch.jackknife_body_mm)} mm.

## Load cases

Mass in kg and the centre of gravity in mm, in the project datum. The towball load comes from moments about the
axle group centre (MASS-04). The last three columns add the growth allowance from report 02 (MASS-06): 20 % on an
estimate, 7 % on a calculated mass and nothing on a maker's figure.

<Trailer kind="cases" />

The car's rear axle, by report 07's method with two adults, the rear seats out, a 30 kg towbar and the fully
loaded towball, carries {n(t.car.rear_kg)} kg before any gear, against its 1,600 kg limit. The car can then carry
{n(t.car.gear_budget_kg)} kg of gear before its {t.car.binds} binds.

## Checks

The towball limits are design targets from report 07, not legal limits. 280 kg is the car's rating, used as a
ceiling, and 6 % of the trailer's mass is the floor set for stability. A failed check shows in red. The mass
checks fail with the growth allowance: the trailer is at its ATM fully loaded before any allowance is added. The
levers are set out in `TRAILER.md`, and none of them is taken here.

## Body length

The build lays out all three body lengths from the same blocks and solves the axle for each. Room for both is
the inside length less the 1,840 mm module.

The 5,500 mm body is the shortest that holds every zone. The 5,000 mm body is short, and nothing has been dropped
to make it fit. These options would shorten the zones. Each is the owner's decision.

### Crossmembers

<Trailer kind="crossmembers" />

No outrigger can pass over the tyres, because the floor is only 50 mm above them at full bump. So outboard of the
rails nothing supports the floor under the garage. It has to span from the rails to the side walls, or hang from
the walls, while it carries the bikes' wheels and the chocks.
