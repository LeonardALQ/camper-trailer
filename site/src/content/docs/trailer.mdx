<a href={withBase('/tow-vehicle/')}>LandCruiser 79</a>, so the trailer has no garage. A lift bed spans the front over
the desk, the kitchen runs along the road side with the battery plinth on the kerb side, a wet room spans the rear,
and a fixed roof tops the {n(t.body_length)} mm body (decisions H1 to H17). The <a href={withBase('/chassis/')}>chassis</a>
and the <a href={withBase('/body/')}>body</a> are designed to planning level, and the other systems are blocks with a
mass. The design document is `TRAILER.md`, and drawing CT-001 is the general arrangement.

## The layout and why

From the front: the drawbar with its box, the bed zone ({n(t.zones.bed)} mm), the living area ({n(t.zones.living)} mm)
and the rear zone ({n(t.zones.rear)} mm), {n(t.zones.interior)} mm inside in all.

- Drawbar box: sealed and vented, {P.dbx_t} mm aluminium, {n(P.dbx_w)} mm wide and {n(P.dbx_h)} mm high on the
  drawbar members. It holds the two Honda EU22i generators and the petrol and diesel jerry cans, so no fuel rides in
  the body. The spare wheel stands on the front wall above it, high enough to lift off over the box.
- Bed and desk: the lift bed spans the front on rails on both side walls. By day its platform's underside is
  1,600 mm up, over the desk and the chair, and at night it comes down over the desk (BED-01). The rest of its
  footprint stays empty (H1, H16).
- Living area: the kitchen runs along the road side, the Combi, fridge and oven in the tower and the hob over the
  freezer and the sink in the bench. On the kerb side the battery plinth stands 125 mm off the wall with the washer
  and the dryer on it, and the entry door is in the kerb wall.
- Partition and wet room: the partition carries the 48 V board at its kerb end and the inverter and the split's
  indoor unit at its road end. The wet room spans the rear, with the DauerShower over its floor well on the road
  side, the cassette toilet against the kerb wall, and its own door in the rear wall, hinged on its kerb edge. The
  split's outdoor unit sits high on the rear wall's road half.
- Under the floor: the fresh tanks sit between the rails in the suspension bays and the grey tank in the rear bay,
  so the towball changes little as the water is used.

The fixed roof carries the solar panels, and the inside is {n(P.interior_h)} mm high. The floor height follows from the
running gear (report 06) and the tyre (report 16): the tyre is {n(ch.tyre_d)} mm across, the hubs sit at
{n(ch.hub_z)} mm and the XT suspension rises 60 mm at full bump, so with {n(ch.tyre_floor_clearance)} mm over the tyre
the floor underside is at {n(ch.floor_underside_z)} mm and the floor surface at {n(ch.floor_z)} mm. The travel height is
{n(ch.travel_height)} mm. The <a href={withBase('/decisions/')}>decision record</a> gives the reasons for the layout.

## Checks

The trailer is registered in NSW. The examiner caps GTM at the lowest of the tyre, coupling, chain and brake ratings
({n(t.caps.gtm)} kg here) and ATM at the lower of the coupling and chain ratings ({n(t.caps.atm)} kg). The plate is
{n(t.caps.plated)} kg (decision H2, amended 23 Nov 2026 from 3,500 kg), inside the Ranger Super Duty's 4,500 kg braked towing and the DO45's 4,500 kg, and fully loaded with growth the trailer stays at least 100 kg under it (MAS-10). Over 3.5 t the chains are grade 80 to AS 2321 and the coupling is rated by its D and V values (COU-01). A failed check shows in red. Limits are never
changed to pass a check, and a part that fails is resized.
