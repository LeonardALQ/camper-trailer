<a href={withBase('/tow-vehicle/')}>LandCruiser 79</a> (see the <a href={withBase('/ute-garage/')}>Bike box</a> page),
so the trailer has no garage. It is layout 1 of the layout study: a queen bed across the front, a fixed roof and a
{n(t.body_length)} mm body. The <a href={withBase('/chassis/')}>chassis</a> and the <a href={withBase('/body/')}>body</a>
are designed to planning level. The other systems are blocks with a mass. The design notes are in `TRAILER.md` in
the repository, and drawing CT-001 is the general arrangement.

## The layout and why

From front to back there are the drawbar with its box, the bed zone ({n(t.zones.bed)} mm), the living area
({n(t.zones.living)} mm) and the rear zone ({n(t.zones.rear)} mm). The inside of the body is {n(t.zones.interior)} mm long.

- **Drawbar box.** A sealed 2 mm aluminium box, {n(P.dbx_w)} mm wide and {n(P.dbx_h)} mm high, sits on the drawbar
  members ahead of the spare wheel. It holds the Honda EU22i generator and the petrol and diesel jerry cans, so no
  fuel rides inside the body. The jockey wheel clamps to the drawbar ahead of it at X 1,000 mm and swings up for
  travel to lie along the drawbar, below the bike box's sweep.
- **Bed zone.** A queen bed lies across the front, from side to side, on a honeycomb base. The kitchen slides out under
  it on the kerb side, under the awning. The air-conditioner sits under the middle of the bed, and a column of
  shelves stands at the bed's road-side end.
- **Living area.** The entry door is in the kerb-side wall. The desk with two seats is on the road side opposite.
  The fridge and the freezer stand on the kerb side behind the door.
- **Rear zone.** The wet bathroom is in the road-side rear corner. The washer, the diesel heater and the Victron
  board stand in front of it, and a tall cupboard fills the kerb-side rear corner.
- **Under the floor.** Two water tanks sit between the rails over the axles, so the towball barely changes as the
  water is used. The batteries sit in a sealed box near the back, and the heater's 15 L diesel tank sits in its own
  bay under the bed.

The fixed roof carries the solar panels. With no raising roof, the inside is {n(P.interior_h)} mm high. The floor
height comes from the running gear in report 06 and the ute's tyre from report 16. The tyre is {n(ch.tyre_d)} mm
across, the hubs sit at {n(ch.hub_z)} mm, and the XT suspension rises 60 mm at full bump. With 50 mm of clearance
above the tyre, the floor underside is at {n(ch.floor_underside_z)} mm and the floor surface at {n(ch.floor_z)} mm. The
travel height is {n(ch.travel_height)} mm.

Layout 2, with the queen bed along the trailer at the back so each sleeper gets out on their own side, is the fallback. It is 830 mm longer and 137 kg heavier (estimate), and
`TRAILER.md` lists it under the open questions.

## Appliances and equipment

Research report 13 chose the products. Three owner choices are defaults until the owner answers, and each can be
reversed in the data: a washer with no dryer, no LPG, and two batteries.

- Bathroom: Thetford C223-CS cassette toilet and a DauerShower 2.0 recirculating shower, imported.
- Washer: Camec 3.5 kg top loader. Fridge: Dometic NRX 130C, 126 L. Freezer: Dometic CFX3 55IM, 53 L.
- Heater and hot water: Truma Combi D 6 on diesel. Air-conditioner: Truma Saphir Comfort RC under the bed.
- Kitchen: Dometic CVI1525 induction hob and CVS1350G sink on Accuride 9301 slides.
- Power at 24 V: a Victron MultiPlus-II 24/5000, two SmartSolar MPPT 150/45, an Orion-Tr charger from the ute and two Lithium NG 25.6 V 200 Ah batteries.
- Solar: four Sunman eArc 430 W glass-free panels bonded to the fixed roof.
- Generator: Honda EU22i, in the drawbar box.

## The ute and the combination

The ute is the {tv.name}. Its limits come from the ute model on the <a href={withBase('/ute-garage/')}>Bike box</a> page. The trailer is checked against
them here:

- Towball: at most {n(tv.limits.towball_plan_kg)} kg (the decision log's cap, inside the towbar's {n(tv.limits.towball_rating_kg)} kg). The worst case with growth carries {n(chk('MAS-03').measured)} kg (MAS-03).
- GCM: the larger of two sums against Toyota's {n(tv.limits.gcm_kg)} kg. The plan's sum is the ute at its {n(tv.limits.weighed_max_kg)} kg limit plus the {n(t.caps.plated)} kg plate less the 250 kg the ball carries. The model's sum is the ute's {tv.gcm_case} case with growth ({n(tv.ute_kg_growth)} kg) plus the heaviest trailer case with growth. GCM-01 measures {n(chk('GCM-01').measured)} kg.
- Coupling height: the ute's ball is {n(ch.coupling_z)} mm high laden (UTE-01), and the drawbar drop is solved from it.
- Jack-knife: the ute's bike box overhangs the ball by {n(ch.sweep.body_overhang_mm)} mm. At 90 degrees it sweeps a radius of {ch.sweep.radius_mm.toLocaleString("en-AU", { minimumFractionDigits: 1, maximumFractionDigits: 1 })} mm from the ball. JK-01 keeps the trailer's front parts at least 100 mm outside that sweep, and JK-02 keeps the parts inside it at least 50 mm below the box's underside at Z {n(ch.sweep.underside_z)}.

## Checks

The trailer is to be registered in NSW. The examiner caps GTM at the lowest of the tyre, coupling, chain and brake
ratings ({n(t.caps.gtm)} kg here) and ATM at the lower of the coupling and chain ratings ({n(t.caps.atm)} kg). The design
plates it lower, at {n(t.caps.plated)} kg, so the combination stays inside the ute's GCM. A failed check shows in red.
The limits stay as they are, and a part that fails is resized.
