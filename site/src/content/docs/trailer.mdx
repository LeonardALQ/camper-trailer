This is the whole trailer, revision J, the home trailer. The motorbikes now ride in an enclosed box on the
<a href={withBase('/tow-vehicle/')}>LandCruiser 79</a> (see the <a href={withBase('/ute-garage/')}>Bike box</a> page),
so the trailer has no garage. It follows the home layout study (decisions H1 to H17): a lift bed across the front over
the desk, the kitchen on the road side and the battery plinth on the kerb side, a wet room across the rear, a fixed
roof and a {n(t.body_length)} mm body. The <a href={withBase('/chassis/')}>chassis</a> and the <a href={withBase('/body/')}>body</a>
are designed to planning level. The other systems are blocks with a mass. The design notes are in `TRAILER.md` in
the repository, and drawing CT-001 is the general arrangement.

## The layout and why

From front to back there are the drawbar with its box, the bed zone ({n(t.zones.bed)} mm), the living area
({n(t.zones.living)} mm) and the rear zone ({n(t.zones.rear)} mm). The inside of the body is {n(t.zones.interior)} mm long.

- **Drawbar box.** A sealed, vented 2 mm aluminium box, {n(P.dbx_w)} mm wide and {n(P.dbx_h)} mm high, sits on the drawbar
  members. It holds the two Honda EU22i generators and the petrol and diesel jerry cans, so no fuel rides inside the
  body. The spare wheel stands on the body's front wall above it, high enough to lift off over the box.
- **Bed and desk.** The lift bed spans the front from side to side on rails on both side walls. By day it rises to
  1,600 mm under its platform, over the desk and the chair space, and at night it comes down over the desk (BED-01).
  The rest of its footprint stays empty (H1, H16).
- **Living area.** The kitchen tower and bench run along the road side: the Combi, the fridge and the oven in the
  tower, the hob over the freezer and the sink in the bench. On the kerb side the battery plinth stands 125 mm off the
  wall with the washer and the dryer on it, and the entry door is in the kerb wall.
- **Partition and wet room.** The partition carries the 48 V board on its kerb end and the inverter and the split's
  indoor unit on its road end. The wet room spans the rear: the DauerShower over its floor well on the road side, the
  cassette toilet against the kerb wall, and its own door in the rear wall, hinged on its kerb edge. The split's
  outdoor unit sits high on the rear wall's road half.
- **Under the floor.** The fresh tanks sit between the rails in the suspension bays and the grey tank in the rear
  bay, so the towball changes little as the water is used.

The fixed roof carries the solar panels. With no raising roof, the inside is {n(P.interior_h)} mm high. The floor
height comes from the running gear in report 06 and the ute's tyre from report 16. The tyre is {n(ch.tyre_d)} mm
across, the hubs sit at {n(ch.hub_z)} mm, and the XT suspension rises 60 mm at full bump. With 50 mm of clearance
above the tyre, the floor underside is at {n(ch.floor_underside_z)} mm and the floor surface at {n(ch.floor_z)} mm. The
travel height is {n(ch.travel_height)} mm.

The <a href={withBase('/decisions/')}>decision record</a> gives the reasons for the layout's main choices.

## Appliances and equipment

Research reports 13 and 19 to 22 chose the products for the home trailer. Every line of the mass schedule below names
its product and source.

- Wet room: Thetford C223-CS cassette toilet and a DauerShower 2.0 recirculating shower over a sealed floor well.
- Laundry: Camec Compact RV II 3.5 kg washer and a Haier 7 kg heat pump dryer on the battery plinth.
- Kitchen: Dometic NRX 130C fridge, Dometic CFX3 55IM freezer, Dometic CVI1525 induction hob, CVS1350G sink and a Camec 28 L convection microwave.
- Heat and hot water: Truma Combi D 6 E on diesel. Cooling: a Mitsubishi Electric 2.5 kW split system.
- Power at 48 V: five Victron Lithium NG 51.2 V 100 Ah batteries (25.6 kWh), a MultiPlus-II 48/6k5, two SmartSolar MPPT 150/35, and Orion-Tr converters for the 12 V and 24 V buses.
- Solar: four Sunman eArc 430 W glass-free panels bonded to the fixed roof.
- Generators: two Honda EU22i in parallel, in the drawbar box.

- Coupling height: the ute's ball is {n(ch.coupling_z)} mm high laden (UTE-01), and the drawbar drop is solved from it.

## Checks

The trailer is to be registered in NSW. The examiner caps GTM at the lowest of the tyre, coupling, chain and brake
ratings ({n(t.caps.gtm)} kg here) and ATM at the lower of the coupling and chain ratings ({n(t.caps.atm)} kg). The design
plates it lower, at {n(t.caps.plated)} kg, so the combination stays inside the ute's GCM. A failed check shows in red.
The limits stay as they are, and a part that fails is resized.
