The body is a box of bonded sandwich panels on the chassis. A sandwich panel is two thin glass-fibre skins bonded to a
light foam core. The floor is a 30 mm panel with woven-roving skins on a PVC foam core, the walls are 35 mm panels with
gelcoat skins on XPS foam, and the raising roof is a 40 mm lid on canvas walls, lifted 825 mm by four electric
actuators. Aluminium extrusions join every panel edge. The body bolts to the chassis at every station through the floor
bearer strip, and it is not bonded to the steel. The design starts from research report 12 and is set out in `BODY.md`.

This is a planning-level proposal for the engineer to check.
