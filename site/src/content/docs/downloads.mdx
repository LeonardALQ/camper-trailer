## 3D models

- <a href={withBase('/downloads/trailer.step')}>trailer.step</a>: the whole trailer as STEP AP214, in the project datum. The tree runs by system: chassis and drawbar, coupling and drawbar fittings, drawbar box, running gear, spare wheel, under the floor, body, roof, bed, living area, electrical, HVAC and ventilation, and wet room.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/trailer.glb')}>trailer.glb</a>: the same as binary glTF. Each node carries its line in the mass schedule.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/chassis.step')}>chassis.step</a>: the chassis and drawbar on their own as STEP AP214, every member as a tube at its true size, with the plates and fittings.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/body.step')}>body.step</a>: the body on its own with its fixed roof and the solar panels, as STEP AP214.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/ute.step')}>ute.step</a>: the Ford Ranger Super Duty single cab chassis as envelope solids with the bike box on it, travelling, in the ute frame DIM-U1 (X rearward from the front axle, Y to the road side, Z up from the ground). The tree runs: cab chassis and accessories, then the bike box by group.

The trailer files use the project datum DIM-01 (research/02): X rearward from the ball, Y positive to the road side
and Z up. It is a right-handed frame, so the files show the real trailer, with the entry door on the kerb side.
Coordinates match the data and the site.
