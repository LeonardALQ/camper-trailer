## 3D model

- <a href={withBase('/downloads/garage-bed.step')}>garage-bed.step</a>: STEP AP214 for any CAD program. Products are named by part number (for example B-04 for a beam), with colours and the assembly tree: module, then bikes, restraint, bed, partitions, garage safety and context. The loading ramp and the ground are left out.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/garage-bed.glb')}>garage-bed.glb</a>: binary glTF for viewers and 3D tools. Each node carries its part number, mass, centre of gravity and mass maturity in its extras.

- <a href={withBase('/downloads/trailer.step')}>trailer.step</a>: the whole-trailer block layout as STEP AP214, in the project datum, with the garage and bed module placed in it. The tree runs by system: chassis and drawbar, coupling and drawbar fittings, running gear, spare wheel, under the floor, body, roof, living area, garage and bed module, garage stowage and payload.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/trailer.glb')}>trailer.glb</a>: the same as binary glTF.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/chassis.step')}>chassis.step</a>: the chassis and drawbar on their own as STEP AP214, every member as a tube at its true size, with the plates and fittings.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/ute.step')}>ute.step</a>: the LandCruiser 79 as envelope solids with the bike garage on it, travelling, in the ute frame DIM-U1 (X rearward from the front axle, Y to the road side, Z up from the ground). The tree runs: cab chassis and accessories, then the bike garage by group.

The files use the project datum DIM-01 (research/02): X rearward from the ball, Y positive to the road side and Z up. That is a right-handed frame, so the files show the real trailer, with the gas locker on the left when you look from behind. Coordinates match the data and the site.

## Bill of materials

- <a href={withBase('/downloads/garage-bed-bom.csv')}>garage-bed-bom.csv</a>: one row per BOM line with quantity, mass, source, maturity, centre of gravity, links and notes.
- <a href={withBase('/downloads/chassis-cut-list.csv')}>chassis-cut-list.csv</a>: the chassis cut list, one row per member and plate with its section, length and mass. Draft until the engineer reviews CHASSIS.md.
