Leonard has replaced the Porsche Cayenne with a Toyota LandCruiser 79 single cab V8. It carries both bikes in an
enclosed, lockable box on the back, so the trailer could be made smaller and lighter. The ute runs a Lovells
4,200 kg GVM kit. GVM (gross vehicle mass) is the most the loaded ute may weigh. The box is a one-piece welded
aluminium body on its own subframe, from research report 17. The design basis is in `UTE-GARAGE.md`.

This is a planning-level proposal for the engineer to check. The trailer no longer has a garage. It is now the
smaller layout on the <a href={withBase('/trailer/')}>Whole trailer</a> page, and the vehicle work is on the
<a href={withBase('/tow-vehicle/')}>Tow vehicle</a> page.

## How it is built

- **Body.** The walls and front are 2.5 mm 5052 aluminium sheet and the roof is 2.0 mm, TIG-welded to a frame of
  40 x 40 x 3 mm square tube. The floor is a 4 mm plate. Two wheel tubs stand 86.5 mm above it, so the floor can sit
  low on the frame rails. The tubs keep Toyota's clearances round the tyre at full bump (estimate).
- **Subframe.** Two aluminium runners lie on the frame rails on rubber strips, with crossmembers and outriggers
  between and outside them. Under each L-track row a galvanised steel crossmember bolts straight to a pair of
  Toyota's deck mount brackets, so the strap anchors reach the frame through steel. Nothing is drilled or welded on
  the frame.
- **Mounting.** The box bolts to Toyota's six pairs of deck mount brackets. The front two pairs are firm, with two
  M12 bolts each. The rear four pairs are compliant, with one M12 bolt through rubber cushions, so the frame can twist
  under the box.
- **Doors.** Two barn-door leaves close the back. The kerb-side leaf shoots bolts into the header and the sill from
  inside. The road-side leaf closes over it on a full-height cam bar, and an ABLOY PL362 padlock on a shrouded hasp
  locks the cam handle. A lift-up door on the kerb side reaches the Street Triple's straps.
- **Security.** A Teltonika FMC130 tracker with door switches and a siren, a Monimoto 9 tracker in each bike, a disc
  lock on each front disc, and a chain from each bike to a ground anchor.
- **Restraint.** As in the retired trailer garage: a Condor chock for each front wheel, two flush L-track rows, LC 1,000 kg
  straps and the WARN winch. LC (lashing capacity) is a strap's rated working load.
- **Tools.** Two chests ride on the wheel tubs, low in the box and ahead of the ramp. The outer front straps run
  through the box's front corners, so the chests cannot go there.

## The ute

Case UC3 is the heaviest: towing the trailer with both bikes in the box and 250 kg on the towball. The static
stability factor (SSF) is half the track divided by the centre of gravity height. A lower SSF means a higher risk of
rolling over.

## Checks

The clearance and contact checks are measured on the 3D model. Clearance limits come from the retired trailer garage's design, and
the tyre clearances are Toyota's. A fail check that misses its limit fails the build. A reported check is shown and
never fails it.

<Ute kind="garage-checks" />

### Deck mount loads

Report 03's load cases with its off-road factor of 2: braking at 1.6 g, cornering at 1.0 g and a net 1.0 g upward, on
the box and everything in it with the growth allowance. Toyota publishes no capacity for its brackets, so the
engineer confirms them.
