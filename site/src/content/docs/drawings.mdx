The drawings come from the same 3D model and data as the rest of the site. Each sheet is A3 in third-angle
projection with dimensions in millimetres, a title block and a revision table, and the assembly sheets have a parts
list. The charts, schematics and schedules (CT-002, CT-601, CT-611) are not to scale (NTS).

Every live sheet is a draft, at the revision the table gives. On a sheet, a revision cloud and a triangle with the
revision letter mark what changed. Sheets CT-401, CT-402 and CT-501 drew the retired garage and bed module and stay
in the register with a note. Nobody has checked or approved the drawings and the engineer has not reviewed them, so
do not build from them. A sheet goes to the shed only once its status is FOR CONSTRUCTION.
