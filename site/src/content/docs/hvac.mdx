Research report 19 sized the cooling and heating from an hourly load calculation of the body
(`research/models/19/loads.py`) and chose the fresh-air system (decision H7). The parts and their prices are in the
<a href={withBase('/trailer/#parts-list')}>trailer's parts list</a>, and the energy is on the
<a href={withBase('/electrical/')}>Electrical</a> page.

The cabin stays fresh with the windows shut. Two heat-recovery pairs exchange the air through MERV 13 filters. A
filtered top-up fan pushes in a little more than the extracts take out, so the inside sits about 3 Pa above outside
and dust stays out, and a relief valve low in the rear wall lets the excess out through the wet room. While towing or
in a dust storm the RESPA-CF2 takes over with its powered precleaner and holds about 20 Pa. CO₂ and humidity sensors
on the Cerbo GX boost the fans when either rises.
