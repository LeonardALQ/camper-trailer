This site holds the design of a one-off off-road camper trailer and the vehicle that tows it. The owner is designing
both and will build them himself in Australia. The trailer will be built to VSB1, the Australian guide for light
trailers, then inspected for registration and reviewed by an engineer.

The tow vehicle is a Toyota LandCruiser 79 single cab V8. It carries the two motorcycles and the tools in an enclosed,
lockable box on its tray. The trailer then needs no garage, so it is smaller and lighter: a 4,420 mm body with a queen
bed across the front, a fixed roof, and the generator and fuel in a box on the drawbar. Most of its life is highway
towing, plus grass, desert tracks, fire trails, light mud and shallow river crossings.

Everything on this site comes from one set of data files. The 3D models, the checks, the mass schedules, the
drawings and the parts lists are generated from them, so they always agree. When a number changes in the data, the
whole site is rebuilt.

## The constraints

- The trailer body is 2,400 mm wide at most. That is a hard limit. With 35 mm walls it leaves 2,330 mm inside.
- The trailer is plated at 3,100 kg ATM, so the combination stays inside the ute's 6,900 kg GCM with the ute at its 3,850 kg limit.
- The towball carries at most 250 kg, the decision log's cap for the ute's rear axle. The design aims for 220 kg fully loaded on base weights, so the growth allowance stays inside the cap.
- The ute's ball sits 440 mm above the ground laden, plus or minus 20 mm, so the coupling stays inside VSB1's 460 mm.
- The bike box is 1,850 mm wide outside at most and weighs less than 440 kg empty.

## What is done

- Research reports 01 to 17, under Research in the menu. Reports 07 and 10 studied the Porsche Cayenne and are superseded by reports 14 to 16 on the LandCruiser 79.
- The garage and bed module, its bill of materials and drawings CT-401, CT-402 and CT-501. It is now retired: its restraint, chocks, straps, winch and ramp moved to the ute's bike box.
- The chassis and drawbar to planning level (research report 11, `CHASSIS.md`): every member, joint and fitting, a 3D frame analysis of every load case, fatigue at each welded detail, and drawings CT-101 to CT-103.
- The appliances, equipment and energy system (research report 13), built into the trailer.
- The LandCruiser 79 and its enclosed bike box (research report 17, `UTE-GARAGE.md`): the ute's axle loads, towball, GCM and stability, the box's 3D model and checks, and drawings CT-701 to CT-704.
- The vehicle plan (`VEHICLE.md`): engine, driveline, GVM kit, brakes, wheels, towing, the legal path, the budget, the order of work and every part, on the <a href={withBase('/tow-vehicle/')}>Tow vehicle</a> page.
- The trailer redesigned for the 79 (`TRAILER.md` revision H): layout 1 with the queen bed across the front, a fixed roof and a 4,420 mm body, the generator and fuel in a drawbar box, checked at the 3,100 kg plate against the ute's limits and the bike box's jack-knife sweep. The chassis (revision D) and the body (`BODY.md` revision B, research report 12) are re-solved for it, and CT-001, CT-101 to CT-103 and CT-201 to CT-301 are drawn for it. Every fail check passes.

## What is next

- Buy the truck and weigh it, then build and weigh the bike box body before choosing the GVM kit (`VEHICLE.md`, steps 1 to 7).
- Confirm the climb over the bed. Layout 2, with the bed along the trailer at the back, is the fallback (`TRAILER.md` open question 2).
- Have the engineer check the chassis, the body and the bike box, starting with the lists at the end of `CHASSIS.md`, `BODY.md` and `UTE-GARAGE.md`.

## How products are chosen

Each product is chosen in this order. First comes reliability and a record of hard use, for example in race
trailers, toy haulers, boats or overland caravans. Next comes parts and service, in Australia if possible. Then
comes serviceability, so a part can be repaired or swapped, ideally in the field. Only then does weight decide,
and the lightest product that passes the first three wins. Price is ignored for the trailer. The vehicle plan has a
budget.
