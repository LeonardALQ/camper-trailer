The design of a one-off off-road home trailer and the LandCruiser 79 that tows it, both to be built in Australia.
It has three parts:

- Car build: a used Toyota LandCruiser 79 single cab V8, rebuilt in stages into a reliable tow vehicle.
- Bike box: an enclosed, lockable box on the ute's tray for the two motorcycles and the tools.
- Trailer: a 4,420 mm body with a lift bed over the desk across the front, a wet room across the rear, a fixed roof,
  and the generators and fuel in a box on the drawbar. It is built to VSB1, the Australian guide for light trailers,
  inspected for registration and reviewed by an engineer. Most of its life is highway towing, with grass, desert
  tracks, fire trails, light mud and shallow river crossings.

Each part has its plan, parts list, 3D model, drawings and measurements under its name in the menu. The models,
checks, mass schedules, drawings, parts lists and costs are all generated from one set of data files, so they agree.

## The constraints

- The trailer body is at most 2,400 mm wide, so 2,330 mm inside with 35 mm walls.
- The trailer is plated at 3,500 kg ATM (decision H2). The ute with growth plus that plate is {aud(gcm.measured)} kg, inside the 7,400 kg GCM of Lovells' revision (decision H20). If the revision is refused, Toyota's 6,900 kg applies and the trailer is plated lower.
- The towball carries at least 8.0 % of the trailer's mass in every case and at most 320 kg with growth (decision H17), inside the towbar's 350 kg. The ute's Lovells Elite kit, plated at a 2,800 kg rear axle for its 1,400 kg wheels (decision H18), keeps its rear axle within that plate at this ball.
- The ute's ball sits 440 mm above the ground laden, plus or minus 20 mm, so the coupling stays inside VSB1's 460 mm.
- The bike box's body is 1,850 mm wide outside at most, its rear tub flares at most 1,910 mm (decision H19), and it weighs less than 440 kg empty.

## How products are chosen

In this order: reliability and a record of hard use (race trailers, toy haulers, boats, overland caravans), then
parts and service in Australia, then field serviceability, then mass, so the lightest product that passes the first
three wins. Price is recorded and decides only between two otherwise equal products. The car build has a budget.
