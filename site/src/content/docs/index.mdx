The design of a one-off off-road home trailer and the Ford Ranger Super Duty that tows it, in Australia.
It has three parts:

- Tow vehicle: a near-new Ford Ranger Super Duty single cab chassis, bought and fitted out in stages.
- Bike box: an enclosed, lockable box on the truck's chassis for the two motorcycles and the tools.
- Trailer: a 4,420 mm body with a lift bed over the desk across the front, a wet room across the rear, a fixed roof,
  and the generators and fuel in a box on the drawbar. It is built to VSB1, the Australian guide for light trailers,
  inspected for registration and reviewed by an engineer. Most of its life is highway towing, with grass, desert
  tracks, fire trails, light mud and shallow river crossings.

Each part has its plan, parts list, 3D model, drawings and measurements under its name in the menu. The models,
checks, mass schedules, drawings, parts lists and costs are all generated from one set of data files, so they agree.

## The constraints

- The trailer body is at most 2,400 mm wide, so 2,330 mm inside with 35 mm walls.
- The trailer's plate goes from 3,500 kg to 4,000 kg ATM (23 Nov 2026), inside the Ranger Super Duty's 4,500 kg braked towing. The truck with growth plus the trailer's plate stays inside Ford's 8,000 kg GCM, {aud(gcm.measured)} kg in the current model (GCM-01).
- The towball carries at least 8.0 % of the trailer's mass in every case (decision H17), within the coupling's and the truck's ratings, and the truck's rear axle stays within Ford's 2,800 kg at that ball.
- The truck's tow pin sits 440 mm above the ground laden, plus or minus 20 mm, so the coupling stays inside VSB1's 460 mm.
- The bike box stays inside Ford's 1,960 mm body width for the single cab chassis (Ford's body equipment mounting manual) and weighs less than 440 kg empty.

## How products are chosen

In this order: reliability and a record of hard use (race trailers, toy haulers, boats, overland caravans), then
parts and service in Australia, then field serviceability, then mass, so the lightest product that passes the first
three wins. Price is recorded and decides only between two otherwise equal products. The truck's upgrades have a budget.
