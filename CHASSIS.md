### The corner steadies

## Analysis

The frame is a 3D beam model on the members' centroid axes, solved by the direct stiffness method in `cad/frame.py`, with 279 nodes, 231 beam elements, the shear panel's two diagonals and 92 rigid links. `cad/tests/test_frame.py` checks the solver against closed forms: cantilevers in bending and torsion, a simply supported beam and the equilibrium of a 3D portal frame.

- The composite rail is one beam with the combined section: centroid at Z 733, I = 18.4 x 10⁶ mm⁴, Z = 135.5 x 10³ mm³ at the bottom and 130.1 x 10³ mm³ at the top, with the 4 mm rail. `cad/tests/test_chassis.py` pins it.
- Crossmembers, outriggers, braces and the drawbar members are beams on their own axes, joined to the rail through rigid links, so every joint is rigid. The drawbar's overlap is linked to the rail at every node along its 1,000 mm.
- The coupling is a ball joint. Each XT arm is a free body: its tyre force, its weight and the airbag force balance about the hinge pivot, 23 mm ahead of the hinge crossmember's rear face and 99 mm below the rail underside. Each pivot is tied to its crossmember at the hinge bar's three 80 mm weld runs a side (est., centred 50, 150 and 250 mm from the rail's inner face), as Cruisemaster builds it, and to the rail through its cheeks. The bar is rigid from the pivot up to the cheeks' 26 mm fillets to it, the cheeks are pinned there, 13 mm under the rail, since those fillets pass a force but no moment, and they run as one member from the pin to the rail's underside, held there by their welds on the web (revision R). The gussets are members.
- The rear tank's shear panel is two diagonals across its bay with the panel's shear stiffness, A = G t L³ / (2 E a b).

Each mass goes onto the frame by its load path, with its resultant and the moment of its offset kept: the floor spans onto the raisers by the lever rule, the roof goes down the side walls to the outriggers, the tanks, battery box and guards hang between the rails, the spare goes to its posts, and the drawbar box to the drawbar members. Block items are cut into 200 mm cells. The ball and the four wheels react each case by statics, and six supports stop only rigid-body motion, with reactions below 1 N. In LC6 and LC6b the frame is held at its hinge pivots and airbag brackets, as VSB1 14 holds the body during the test. In LC8a the ground holds it: each wheel's force along the trailer acts at its hub, unbraked, and across and up at its contact, at the tyres' rates with the trailer a rigid body on them, or with one wheel held fast at its hub and the pull through it (revision Q). The trailer's weight acts with the pull, the forces along and across hold the pull in plan, and the tyres and the nose hold what that leaves through supports that cannot pull, a tyre that would leaving the ground until every support in contact pushes, and the pull taken at the share the trailer holds where it tips first (revision R). The arms carry those forces to the frame as in the road cases, but an airbag cannot pull, so an arm the pull drives down past what its airbag gives at full droop rests on its shock absorber at full extension, which pulls on its crossmember. Stress at each element end combines as √(σ² + 3τ²).
